# Our principles

[← Back to VYAAPAK](README.md)

These principles describe the standards guiding VYAAPAK's development.

## Preserve attribution

Knowledge should retain its context. A traditional statement, a later interpretation, and an observed result should be distinguishable.

## Make reasoning understandable

People should be able to understand the basis of an output. Explanations should help users examine an interpretation and its assumptions.

## Connect learning to capability

Learning objectives should connect to practical application. Progress should reflect skills developed through exercises and feedback.

## Evaluate distinct claims separately

Calculation correctness, faithful representation of a traditional rule, and predictive performance are separate questions.

- **Calculation correctness:** whether a calculation follows its stated method.
- **Source fidelity:** whether an interpretation accurately represents its attributed tradition or source.
- **Predictive performance:** how recorded forecasts compare with observed outcomes under a defined evaluation method.

Evidence for one does not establish the others. This showcase makes no claim of independently established predictive accuracy.

## Respect privacy

Public product communication should use purpose-made demonstration material. Client records and private research data do not belong in a public showcase.

## Report substance

Public updates should describe meaningful changes. Performance claims should identify the evaluation method, sample, and limitations.
