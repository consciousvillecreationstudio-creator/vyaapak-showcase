## The mark

An upright serif **V within a diamond outline**, adapted from the VYAAPAK Studio frontend. Use the same core mark across public product materials.

## The name and theme

**VYAAPAK**

**Jyotish, baked in technology, based in scriptures.**

## Visual treatment

White backgrounds, dark ink, restrained plum accents, and clear typography. Keep the mark legible, with enough surrounding space, and preserve its proportions.
