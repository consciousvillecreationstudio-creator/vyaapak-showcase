# The VYAAPAK ecosystem

[← Back to VYAAPAK](README.md)

VYAAPAK brings the mathematics, literature, and interpretation of Jyotish into one connected ecosystem. Computational engines, source-linked knowledge, and the development of explainable AI support its work across practice, learning, discussion, and research.

## Studio · Your practice workspace

For practitioners who want continuity across chart work and consultations.

Studio brings practice organisation, chart exploration, and consultation activity into a single workspace. The aim is to keep the practical work of Jyotish organised and accessible.

## Marg Darshan · Learning through practice

For learners developing the ability to apply what they study.

Marg Darshan centres learning on defined objectives, practical exercises, feedback, and progression. Studio provides the practice environment for that learning.

## Vidyalaya · Knowledge with context

For people who want concise study material with clear attribution.

Vidyalaya organises knowledge into focused learning units connected to sources and learning objectives. The emphasis is on useful, connected knowledge.

## Vaad · Structured prediction and discussion

For participants exploring questions and comparing reasoning.

Vaad brings scenarios, predictions, and discussion into a structured setting. Its purpose is to support participation and reflection on outcomes.

## Labs · Research and evaluation

For researchers investigating questions and examining evidence.

Labs connects research activity with evidence and evaluation. Its purpose is to support disciplined investigation and clear reporting.

## AI development and future APIs

We’re developing models grounded in structured Jyotish knowledge and outcome-labelled observations. Recorded predictions and feedback support hypothesis testing and model evaluation. APIs are planned for selected capabilities; they are not currently offered through this repository.
